Bicycle Day — how it works

One image, warped by a shader. Sound and picture share one clock. Tables below are read from the app source at build time.

Signal flow

1 · SourcesGenerated music (seeded), a Local file, Live tab audio, or the microphone.buildAudio() · playLocal() · startLive()
2 · FeaturesIntensity, energy, events (kicks, builds, drops, scenes) from the arc or from the audio.score(u) · energy(u) · liveStep() · analyseLocal()
3 · LayersArc, surges, moods, scenes, minor punches and zoom pulses add up into the engine's own look.surgeAt · moodAt · sceneAt · minorAt · zoomPulseAt
4 · POne params object for the frame: every field has a neutral value and a range (PF).render(t, dt, …)
5 · User layerEnergy, control level (auto / assisted / manual), keys and sliders change P.UE.* · ctrlParams(P, t, dt)
6 · clampPForces every field into its PF range, once per frame.clampP(P)
7 · ShadersPass 1 warps, mirrors and feeds back into a buffer. Pass 2 adds aberration, vignette, grain, moods.FS_SCENE (feedback) → FS_SHOW (display)
8 · ScreenThe canvas you see.render() → gl.drawArrays

Effects

Other engine effects (showcase / automatic only, no key)

Energy (the user's one picture setting)

Five levels, read from EN. Look only, the music does not change. off shifts the arc, amp scales every effect, big scales the big moves (tiles, tunnel, glitch, blaze, zoom pulses), strobe scales the strobe mood. Level 4 (extreme) switches the hardcore look on.

Slow start

Music

Piano pieces (calm arc) and generated synth styles. Each style is a branch of buildAudio; bpm is its tempo range, nohb = no heartbeat pulse in the picture.

Invariants

  • The picture is a pure function of the audio clock time t. Drifting state (hue, rotA) is re-derived on seek (integrateTo).
  • Pass 1 (FS_SCENE) is a feedback loop: anything added there builds up frame after frame. One-shot flashes belong in the display pass (FS_SHOW).
  • Strobe is allowed on purpose, but only in the display pass, never in the feedback loop.
  • User keys and sliders are live input: with a control level above auto the picture is no longer a pure function of t.
